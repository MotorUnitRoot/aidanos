# Forensics

This is a short pass over the jobs behind CRM, ERP, and project screens. The costume was dropped. The job was kept.

## CRM

Salesforce Lightning and tools like it put a person or a deal on a record page. The page has a highlights panel, a colored path, related lists, and an activity feed. The job is to see who this is, what state they are in, what is attached, and what to change. The waffle, the utility bar, the brand header, and the path are costume. AidanOS keeps key facts, a person, a status, fields, a related strip, and an attachment link. It refuses the chrome.

## ERP

NetSuite-style work is a master record and its detail lines. A sales order has a header and a sublist of items. The job is to name the thing, enter amounts, and keep the lines honest. Portlets, dashboards, and a second database of lists are costume. AidanOS keeps labeled fields, money, numbers, dates, line items, a table head, and a validation line.

## Project tools

Jira, Linear, and Asana-style tools show one issue and a list of issues. The job is to see the work, who holds it, how long it takes, and what is next. Boards and timelines can wait. Swimlanes, sprint chrome, and a second project database are costume. AidanOS keeps a list row, a filter chip, a duration mark, a progress count, and the same paper as the day.

## How a job is marked

Look at the tired person. What must they see, change, or connect?

If the Door, Today, and Plan library already names that job, the mark is Already.

If the job is needed for generated work and can live on Stoic paper with one serif and no second store, the mark is Keep.

If the job is real but the form is not ready, the mark is Deferred. Board lane and Timeline view wait for a later mark.

If the thing only brands the vendor, forces a second database, opens a theme shop, or asks for a new page shell, the mark is Refused. Refused never ships.
