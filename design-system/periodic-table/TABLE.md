# Periodic table

This is the closed list. Generation may use only Keep and Already. Deferred waits for a later mark. Refused never ships.

Elements are grouped by family. Each entry names the job, where CRM, ERP, and project tools show it, how AidanOS keeps it on Stoic paper, and the mark.

## Identity

### Key facts

What it is: the few facts a tired person needs at the top of a record.

CRM: the highlights panel on an account or opportunity.

ERP: the header fields on a transaction.

Project: the title row of an issue.

AidanOS: a short stack of quiet labels and ink values on 42rem paper. No card. No icon row. No colored path.

Mark: Keep

### Person chip

What it is: who this work belongs to.

CRM: the owner field and the contact name on a record.

ERP: the employee or vendor on a transaction.

Project: the assignee on an issue.

AidanOS: a person’s name as a wiki link or as plain ink. No avatar stack. No presence pip.

Mark: Keep

### Status mark

What it is: the one word that says where the work stands.

CRM: stage or status on an opportunity.

ERP: approval or posting state on a transaction.

Project: issue status.

AidanOS: one word in quiet ink. Not a colored pill. Not a sales path.

Mark: Keep

## Fields

### Labeled field

What it is: a name paired with a value.

CRM: a field on a record layout.

ERP: a field on a form.

Project: a property on an issue.

AidanOS: a quiet label above a value. The pair is the field. It is not a tab and not a second typeface.

Mark: Keep

### Text field

What it is: one line a person can type.

CRM: a text field on a record.

ERP: a short form field.

Project: the issue title or a short property.

AidanOS: one line on paper, same serif, hairline edge. It is not a search chrome and not a chat box.

Mark: Keep

### Multiline field

What it is: several lines a person can type.

CRM: a long text or description field.

ERP: a memo on a transaction.

Project: the issue body.

AidanOS: several lines on the same paper. No rich-text toolbar. What is saved is ordinary words.

Mark: Keep

### Choice field

What it is: one value from a closed list of words.

CRM: a picklist.

ERP: a list field on a form.

Project: priority or type.

AidanOS: a closed list of words on paper. Not a multi-select rainbow. Not a tag cloud.

Mark: Keep

### Date field

What it is: a day a person can set.

CRM: close date or activity date.

ERP: transaction date.

Project: due date.

AidanOS: a date in ordinary words or short numbers. Month day stays the calendar cell. This field is for generated forms.

Mark: Keep

### Number field

What it is: a count a person can set.

CRM: quantity or score.

ERP: quantity on a line.

Project: story points or a count.

AidanOS: tabular numbers in the same serif. Not a slider. Not a gauge.

Mark: Keep

### Money field

What it is: an amount with a currency mark.

CRM: amount on an opportunity.

ERP: rate, total, or tax on a transaction.

Project: a budget or a price on work.

AidanOS: an amount on paper. Not a ledger widget. Not a dashboard tile.

Mark: Keep

### Toggle

What it is: on or off.

CRM: a checkbox that means a state, not a task.

ERP: a flag on a form.

Project: a switch such as blocked or done.

AidanOS: on or off as a word or a square. The task checkbox stays the tick on a line of work. This is not an iOS switch.

Mark: Keep

### Checkbox

What it is: a tick on a line of work.

CRM: a completed activity.

ERP: a received or billed flag on a line.

Project: a done item in a checklist.

AidanOS: the box is the hit target. The words take the caret. A checked box uses the muted green. Already named in the library.

Mark: Already

## Collections

What it is: one line in a collection of records.

CRM: a row in a list view.

ERP: a row in a search or a register.

Project: a row in an issue list.

AidanOS: a name, one fact, and maybe a status, on one line. Not a Salesforce list view. Not a pin, chart, or filter drawer.

Mark: Keep

### Line item

What it is: one detail under a master record.

CRM: a product line on an opportunity.

ERP: a sublist line on an order or bill.

Project: a child row under a piece of work.

AidanOS: a name, a quantity, and money on one line. The header stays key facts. The lines stay on the same paper.

Mark: Keep

### Filter chip

What it is: a word that narrows a list.

CRM: a filter on a list view.

ERP: a saved search chip.

Project: a label or a filter on a list.

AidanOS: a word with a hairline, which a person can dismiss. Not a filter builder. Not a facet cloud.

Mark: Keep

### Table head

What it is: the names of the columns over a list or over line items.

CRM: column headers on a related list.

ERP: column headers on a sublist.

Project: column headers on an issue table.

AidanOS: quiet labels in a row. No sort-arrow chrome. No grip handles.

Mark: Keep

### Hanging list

What it is: a list that hangs from a mark.

CRM: a short list on a record.

ERP: a note list on a form.

Project: a bullet list in an issue.

AidanOS: the mark sits in the gutter. The words hang to the right. Already named in the library.

Mark: Already

### Search hit

What it is: one result from Ask or find.

CRM: a global search row.

ERP: a global search row.

Project: a command or search row.

AidanOS: the file name is quiet. The title is ink. Opening a hit uses the same paper. Already named in the library.

Mark: Already

### Board lane

What it is: a column of cards that means a state.

CRM: a Kanban on a list view.

ERP: a pipeline board.

Project: a board in Jira, Linear, or Asana.

AidanOS: the job is real. The form is not ready. A generated screen uses a list row and a status mark until a later mark.

Mark: Defer

ERP: links from a transaction to a vendor, a customer, or another document.

AidanOS: a row of wiki links on the paper. Not a related list with New, Edit, and View. Not a second store of objects.

Mark: Keep

CRM: a file on a record.

ERP: a file on a transaction.

Project: an attachment on an issue.

AidanOS: a file name that opens the file. The file lives in the vault. Not a Files tab. Not a cloud drive chrome.

Mark: Keep

### Wiki link

What it is: a painted note name that points at another file.

CRM: a lookup to another record.

ERP: a link to another document.

Project: a mention of another issue.

AidanOS: the painted word is the note. Brackets stay hidden while idle. The disk is still a wiki link. Already named in the library.

Mark: Already

What it is: accept or refuse.

CRM: save and cancel on a record.

ERP: submit and cancel on a form.

Project: confirm and dismiss on a dialog.

AidanOS: two hairline buttons. Get to Work, Capture thoughts, Accept, and Reject are the same button. Not a filled brand button. Not a footer bar of chrome.

Mark: Keep

### Door prompt bar

What it is: the Door question as a search-like field.

CRM, ERP, Project: a global ask that opens work.

AidanOS: empty Enter opens Today. It is not a chat thread. Already named in the library.

Mark: Already

### Hairline button

What it is: one button with a quiet edge.

CRM: a primary action on a record.

ERP: a form action.

Project: an issue action.

AidanOS: a hairline box, never underlined text. Already named in the library.

Mark: Already

### Text link

What it is: a quiet word that takes you somewhere.

CRM: a navigation item or a cancel.

ERP: a form link.

Project: a view switch.

AidanOS: Today, Plan, Month, and close. No box and no underline. Already named in the library.

Mark: Already

### Ask strip

CRM: global search.

ERP: global search.

Project: command menu.

AidanOS: the paper scrolls. The strip stays. It is not a mail feed. Already named in the library.

Mark: Already

## Structure

### Hairline rule

What it is: a line that divides the paper.

CRM: a section break on a record.

ERP: a break between header and sublist.

Project: a break between title and body.

AidanOS: one pixel of hairline across the paper. Not a card shadow. Not a colored bar.

Mark: Keep

### 42rem paper

What it is: the writing column.

CRM, ERP, Project: the body of the work.

AidanOS: the column is 42rem and centered. One serif. What is saved is ordinary markdown. Already named in the library.

Mark: Already

### Page shell as a new look

What it is: a new frame around the work.

CRM: the Lightning app shell, with a header, a waffle, a utility bar, and object tabs.

ERP: a console with portlets and a side list of records.

Project: a product chrome with a side nav of projects and a tab set.

AidanOS: generated work stays on the 42rem paper. If a last-mile file would need a new look, the system is wrong. This never ships.

Mark: Refuse

## Deferred

- Board lane
- Timeline view

## Refused

These never ship. They brand a vendor, force a second store, open a theme shop, or ask for a new page shell.

- Page shell as a new look
- Salesforce Lightning chrome: app launcher, utility bar, global header, path, highlights card
- Theme catalog and skin shop
- Second store or second database
- Second typeface for chrome
- Colored status pills and sport underlines
- Tab set as a product shell
- Side nav of objects
- Dashboard widgets and portlets
- Toast stacks and notification bells
- Avatar stacks and presence
- Floating chat
- Dimmed modal as a second room
- Filter builder as a product
- Rich-text toolbar as vendor chrome
- Activity feed as Salesforce activity
- Settings page of chrome
