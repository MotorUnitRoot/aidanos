# Pack a crate

## Why
A made-up map for moving sample panes into a crate. Copy the file to take the map. Delete it to drop it.

## Stages

### 1. Choose the panes
Enter: The shelf is in front of you
Exit: Three panes are chosen
Why: The crate only holds the samples for this drill.

### 2. Wrap
Enter: Three panes are chosen
Exit: Each pane is wrapped
Next steps:
- [ ] Wrap the blue pane
- [ ] Wrap the cracked sample
- [x] Find the paper

### Fork: Room on the shelf / crate is full
Only one
- Room on the shelf → Label
- Crate is full → Label

### 3. Label
Enter: The panes are wrapped
Exit: The crate has a label
Why: The label says this is a sample, not a shipment.
