# Family room to office

## Why
A finished family room that stays living space.

## Stages

### 1. See the room
Enter: You are in the doorway
Exit: The room is the one on the drawing

### 2. Name the use
Enter: The room is named
Exit: It stays living space

### 3. Read the structure
Enter: The walls are in front of you
Exit: You know whether a wall might carry load

### Fork: Wall stays / might carry load
Only one
- Wall stays → Ask the town
- Might carry load → Ask the town

### 4. Ask the town
Enter: The structure is read
Exit: The town has been asked
Why: The town’s answer controls the permit, not this spec.

### Fork: Surface only / open the wall
Only one
- Surface only → Rough
- Open the wall → Rough

### 5. Rough
Enter: The town has been asked
Exit: Rough work is ready for inspection
Why: If the wall opens, rough work and the rough inspection happen before drywall.
Next steps:
- [ ] Rough work before drywall
- [ ] The rough inspection before drywall
- [ ] Cut the wall

### 6. Close and finish
Enter: The room is ready to close
Exit: The finish is on
Why: Paint, flooring in the same place, casing, base, and a simple built-in.
Next steps:
- [ ] Prime
- [ ] Casing and crown
- [ ] The hard floor

### 7. Final
Enter: The finish is on
Exit: The desk can move in
Why: A built-in that sits on the subfloor goes in before the floor. One that sits on the finish floor goes in after. If a permit was pulled, the final inspection is before the desk moves in.
